Who we are

YOUR MONDAY MORNING WILL NEVER BE THE SAME!

That loop is rarely a discipline problem. It is what happens when nobody decided who the content is for, what it promises, or what each piece is meant to prove. Without those three, every week starts from scratch and the account ends up looking like five different businesses.

We are a small studio in Kuala Lumpur. We settle those three things first, then turn them into a month of content you can actually film. One product, one price, four clients at a time.

How a month runs

The same four beats every month, so you always know what is coming and when.

W1 The plan lands Ideas, hooks, scripts, captions, schedule. You approve it, or send one round of changes.
W2 You publish Questions get answered as they come. If the audience reacts to something nobody expected, we follow it.
W3 Mid-month check A short look at the four numbers. If something is dying, you hear about it while there is still time to act.
W4 Report and one change What happened, which topic started real conversations, and the single thing to do differently next month.

The actual work

Take a physiotherapist in KL who treats runners. One line about what she does, turned into a month.

Before

  • Stretch of the week
  • Clinic renovation photos
  • A quote over a sunset
  • Public holiday greetings

Consistent. Pleasant. Could belong to any clinic in the country, and nothing in it says who she is for.

Four of the eight angles

  • The knee pain runners self-diagnose wrong, and what it usually is
  • Why your physio keeps asking about your shoes
  • What she tells a runner six weeks out from a marathon with a niggle
  • The three runners she turns away, and where she sends them

Same clinic, same hour of filming. Now every post is aimed at a runner deciding who to trust with an injury.

Nothing above needs a bigger camera or more hours. It needs somebody to decide who the content is for before anyone hits record. That decision is most of what you are paying for.
