## B. TikTok / YouTube script (45 to 60 seconds)

*Talking head. Alan on camera.*

If every post feels different from the last one, don't fix the posts yet. Check three
things first.

One. Who is it for. Not your whole market, the specific person. If you can't name them
without saying "everyone," every hook you write has to stay vague, and vague gets
scrolled.

Two. What you actually promise. One sentence. Ask two people on your team separately and
see if the answers match. Most of the time they don't, and that gap shows up in the
content before anyone notices it in the business.

Three. What each post is supposed to prove. Reach, trust, or buying intent. Pick one per
post. If you can't say which, the post is decoration.

Fix those three and consistency gets easier, because now there's something to be
consistent about.

## D. The POSITION checklist (the promised resource)

This has to exist before Tuesday, otherwise the CTA is a lie. Keep it to one page.

**Positioning gap check. Five questions, honest answers only.**

1. Who is this content for? Name one person, not a segment. If you wrote "everyone" or
   listed three audiences, stop here. That's the gap.
2. What do you promise them, in one sentence? Now ask two people on your team the same
   question separately. Do the three answers match?
3. What is each post supposed to prove? Pick one per post: reach, trust, or buying intent.
   If a post proves none of them, it's decoration.
4. What are you willing to be known for by December? Work backwards from that instead of
   starting each month with "what should we post?"
5. Is your best performing post connected to what you sell? If not, you're growing an
   audience for a product you don't have.

Score: how many did you answer without hedging? Under three, positioning is the bottleneck
and more content won't move it.
