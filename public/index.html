How a month runs

The same four beats every month, so you always know what is coming and when.

W1 The plan lands Ideas, hooks, scripts, captions, schedule. You approve it, or send one round of changes.
W2 You publish Questions get answered as they come. If the audience reacts to something nobody expected, we follow it.
W3 Mid-month check A short look at the four numbers. If something is dying, you hear about it while there is still time to act.
W4 Report and one change What happened, which topic started real conversations, and the single thing to do differently next month.

After

Every post is written for one reader: a runner with an injury, working out which physio to call. Same clinic, same hour of filming. Open any post to see the script, the caption, and the one thing it asks for.

W1
Teach The knee pain runners self-diagnose wrong, and what it usually is

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. Point at the outside of your knee. If that is where it hurts after a long run, it is usually not the knee itself.
  2. Show where the band actually runs, and what it is doing by kilometre eight.
  3. One thing to stop this week, one thing to start.

Caption

Sore on the outside of the knee after a long run? Tell me which kilometre it starts and I will tell you what I would check first.

One action

Comment the kilometre. She replies in the thread, then moves it to DM.

Point of view Why your physio keeps asking about your shoes

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. Every runner who walks in gets asked about their shoes. Most of them think it is a sales question.
  2. Hold up two pairs at different wear. Read the sole out loud: here is how this person lands.
  3. Tell them what to photograph before a first session.

Caption

Bring your two most-run pairs to a first session. The soles say more than the form you fill in.

One action

Save it for your next appointment.

W2
Teach What she tells a runner six weeks out from a marathon with a niggle

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. Six weeks out, a niggle is a scheduling problem before it is a pain problem.
  2. The three questions she asks before touching anyone's training plan.
  3. What she will not do this close to a race, and why.

Caption

Six weeks out and something is grumbling? The plan changes before the treatment does. Here is the order I work in.

One action

DM the word SIX for the sheet she works from.

Point of view The three runners she turns away, and where she sends them

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. Not every sore knee is hers to treat. Three of them go somewhere else, same day.
  2. Name all three, plainly, and who she picks up the phone to for each.
  3. Why sending someone away is part of the job.

Caption

If you are one of these three, I am not the right call, and you will hear that in the first ten minutes. Here is who is.

One action

Send it to the runner who has been putting off a scan.

W3
Proof What a first session actually looks like, minute by minute

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. Forty minutes, filmed from the doorway, nothing staged.
  2. Talk through each block as it happens: history, gait, hands on, plan.
  3. End on what they walk out with, on paper.

Caption

First session, start to finish, so you know what you are booking before you book it.

One action

Booking link is in the profile.

Teach The warm-up most runners skip, and the one that earns its place

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. Nobody has fifteen minutes. So cut it down to the part that pays for itself.
  2. Do it at speed, in normal clothes, on the pavement outside.
  3. Say how long it takes. Four minutes.

Caption

Four minutes, before your next run. That is the whole ask.

One action

Try it tomorrow, then tell her if the first kilometre felt different.

W4
Point of view Rest is not a treatment plan

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. Two weeks off, then straight back to the same mileage. That is the loop she sees most.
  2. What rest fixes, and what it leaves exactly where it was.
  3. Give the shape of a return instead of a number.

Caption

Rest buys you time. It does not change what caused it. That part is the work.

One action

Comment REST for the return-to-running outline.

Offer Who she can see this month, and what to bring

Script, for a phone and one take

  1. The plain version: a few slots left, these days, these hours.
  2. Who they are best spent on, and who is fine to wait.
  3. What to bring, and what happens in the first ten minutes.

Caption

Slots for this month are on the booking page. Bring your shoes and your last four weeks of runs.

One action

Book it, or DM if you are not sure it is the right fit.
