How a month runs

The same four beats every month, so you always know what is coming and when.

W1 The plan lands Ideas, hooks, scripts, captions, schedule. You approve it, or send one round of changes.
W2 You publish Questions get answered as they come. If the audience reacts to something nobody expected, we follow it.
W3 Mid-month check A short look at the four numbers. If something is dying, you hear about it while there is still time to act.
W4 Report and one change What happened, which topic started real conversations, and the single thing to do differently next month.

What you get

Six things, every month.

01PositioningYour angle, written down, revisited every quarter.
02Eight ideasEach one mapped to one of your content pillars.
03Eight scriptsWritten to be filmed on a phone, by you.
04Eight captionsOne clear action each. No caption ends with nothing to do.
05A scheduleWhat goes out, where, and on which day.
06A report and a callWe go through your four numbers and the one change to make next month.

Not included, on purpose

  • Filming and editing. You produce, or your editor does.
  • Posting for you
  • Running your DMs day to day
  • Paid ads and media buying
  • Unlimited revisions. One round per plan.

Why

  • Leaving production out keeps the work sharp and the price sane.
  • If nobody on your side can film, this is not the right fit yet.

The work

One month, before and after.

Before

A makeup artist in Kuala Lumpur who teaches a weekend class and takes wedding bookings. They posted every week without missing. Nothing in it said who they work with, so someone planning a wedding had no reason to pick them over the artist with more followers.

After

Every post is written for one reader: someone with a wedding in four months, working out whether to book an artist or do their own face. Same artist, same hour of filming.

Teach The step people skip that makes everything slide by noon

Script, for a phone

  1. It is thirty three degrees outside and your face has to last twelve hours. Skip this and nothing else you do matters.
  2. Put it on one side only, on camera, then carry on filming and come back to it.
  3. Say what is worth spending on and what is not.

Caption

Half my face, six hours apart, same day, same weather. The difference is one step and it takes forty seconds.

One action

Comment HALF for the product list.

Point of view Why they ask what time your event ends before anything else

Script, for a phone

  1. Every booking starts with the same question, and it is not about the look you want.
  2. Twelve hours in this weather is a different job from four hours in an air-conditioned hall.
  3. What changes in the kit when the answer is: we finish at midnight.

Caption

Tell me when your event ends and I will tell you what your face needs. It changes more than the look does.

One action

Proof A full trial, start to finish, in real time

Script, for a phone

  1. Ninety minutes cut down, one phone on a tripod, no edit tricks.
  2. Talk through each stage as it happens: skin, base, eyes, setting.

Teach The three photos to take tonight, before you decide anything

Script, for a phone

  1. Your face has to work under a phone flash at eleven at night, not just in daylight.
  2. Take the same photo in three lights, on camera, so the difference is visible.

Caption

One action
